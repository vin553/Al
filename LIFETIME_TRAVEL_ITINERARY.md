# The Lifetime Journey — A Travel Plan from 35 to the End

A complete, year-by-year itinerary built to match **the right place to the right month** and, above all, **the right experience to the right stage of your body and life.** The single most important idea in this whole plan: *do the physically hard, high-altitude, remote, once-in-a-lifetime trips first.* Comfort, culture, and slowness can wait for later decades. Knees, lungs, and altitude tolerance cannot.

## The science this plan is built on

Five evidence-based principles drive the sequencing. This isn't decoration — it's *why* each trip sits where it does.

1. **Front-load physical difficulty (the capacity curve).** VO₂ max, joint resilience, balance, and altitude tolerance all decline with age. Everest Base Camp, Kilimanjaro, Patagonia, multi-day treks, and deep diving belong in your 30s–40s. Pushing them to your 60s turns a peak experience into a medical risk. **This is the non-negotiable backbone of the plan.**

2. **Buy experiences, not things — and space them out.** Decades of research (Gilmore, Gilbert, Dunn) show experiential purchases produce more durable happiness than material ones, partly because we adapt slowly to memories and quickly to objects. Spacing trips out *increases* total joy by preserving anticipation (often the happiest phase of a trip) and preventing hedonic adaptation.

3. **Chase awe.** Keltner's research links awe — the feeling of vastness that reframes the self — to lower stress, more generosity, and a stronger sense of meaning. This plan deliberately stacks awe sources: vast landscapes, deep history, sacred architecture, wildlife at scale, the night sky, the ocean.

4. **Optimize for the memory, not the moment (peak-end rule).** Kahneman showed we remember a trip by its emotional peak and its ending, not its average. So each trip is designed around one unmistakable peak, and longer journeys end on a high rather than a logistical slog.

5. **Seasonality is non-negotiable.** Every destination has a *correct* month — for weather, wildlife migration, festivals, light, and crowd/cost avoidance (shoulder season is usually the sweet spot). Going to the right place in the wrong month is the most common way a great trip becomes a mediocre one. Every entry below names the month and why.

# PHASE I — PEAK ADVENTURE (Ages 35–45 · 2026–2036)

> *Rule of the decade: if a trip needs your lungs, knees, or nerve, do it now. Everything in this phase is hard to do — or impossible — later.*

### Age 35 · 2026
- **Nepal — Everest Base Camp Trek.** City: Kathmandu → Lukla → Gorak Shep. **Month: October** (post-monsoon: clearest skies, stable weather, the Himalaya at its most visible). **Duration: 16 days.** *Experience:* the single greatest high-altitude trek on earth while your altitude tolerance is at its peak — Namche Bazaar, Tengboche monastery, the amphitheatre of 8,000m peaks. This is deliberately trip #1: it's the one most punished by aging.
- **Iceland — Ring Road & Highlands.** City: Reykjavík + Vík + Landmannalaugar. **Month: late June** (midnight sun, highland roads open). **Duration: 10 days.** *Experience:* waterfalls, glaciers, volcanic highlands, puffins — raw geology and endless light. A gentler counterweight to Everest in the same year.

### Age 36 · 2027
- **Tanzania — Kilimanjaro + Serengeti.** City: Moshi (climb) → Serengeti. **Month: late January–February** (dry, good summit weather; calving season on the plains). **Duration: 14 days** (8-day Lemosho route for acclimatization + 5-day safari). *Experience:* Africa's highest summit at dawn, then the Great Migration and big cats. Climb-first while the legs are fresh.
- **Peru — Inca Trail to Machu Picchu + Sacred Valley.** City: Cusco → Machu Picchu. **Month: June** (dry season, clear; just before the July crowds). **Duration: 9 days.** *Experience:* the classic 4-day trail over Dead Woman's Pass to the Sun Gate at sunrise — a trek that gets materially harder with age.

### Age 37 · 2028
- **Patagonia — Torres del Paine + Los Glaciares.** City: Puerto Natales (Chile) + El Chaltén (Argentina). **Month: December–early February** (austral summer; long days, accessible trails). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* the W or O trek, Fitz Roy, Perito Moreno glacier calving. Wind-blasted, demanding, unforgettable — peak-fitness terrain.
- **Japan — Cherry Blossoms & Kumano Kodo.** City: Kyoto + Kii Peninsula. **Month: late March–early April** (sakura). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* temples under blossom, then the ancient Kumano Kodo pilgrimage trail. Beauty + a soulful walk.

### Age 38 · 2029
- **Antarctica — Expedition Cruise (incl. optional camping/kayak).** Port: Ushuaia → Antarctic Peninsula. **Month: late November–early December** (pristine ice, pack ice breaking, penguin courtship). **Duration: 12 days** (incl. Drake Passage). *Experience:* the seventh continent — the most awe-dense place on the planet. Do it while you can handle Zodiac landings and the Drake. (Front-loaded because it's strenuous, costly to repeat, and best experienced with full mobility.)
- **Galápagos — Live-aboard.** City: Baltra → islands. **Month: June** (cooler, active wildlife, calm). **Duration: 8 days.** *Experience:* snorkeling with sea lions, marine iguanas, blue-footed boobies — Darwin's lab, requiring swimming/diving fitness.

### Age 39 · 2030
- **Indonesia — Komodo & Raja Ampat diving.** City: Labuan Bajo + Sorong. **Month: October–November** (dry, best visibility in Raja Ampat). **Duration: 16 days.** *Experience:* the richest marine biodiversity on earth, Komodo dragons, manta rays. Serious diving — squarely a young-body trip.
- **New Zealand — Milford Track & South Island.** City: Queenstown + Fiordland. **Month: December–January** (Great Walk season). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* "the finest walk in the world," Milford Sound, glaciers, adventure sports.

### Age 40 · 2031 — *milestone year, go big*
- **Bhutan — Druk Path Trek + Tiger's Nest.** City: Paro + Thimphu. **Month: October** (clear autumn skies, festivals). **Duration: 11 days.** *Experience:* the high trek and the cliffside Paro Taktsang monastery — a Himalayan kingdom that limits tourism, best done with strong legs.
- **Jordan + Egypt — Petra, Wadi Rum, Nile.** City: Petra + Wadi Rum + Luxor. **Month: November** (mild desert temps). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* Petra on foot, a night in the Wadi Rum desert, the temples of Karnak and the Valley of the Kings. A landmark 40th.

### Age 41 · 2032
- **Norway — Lofoten & Fjords + Northern Lights.** City: Bergen + Lofoten + Tromsø. **Month: late September** (aurora returns, autumn colour, before deep cold). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* fjord hikes, Lofoten's peaks, first aurora hunt.
- **Vietnam — North to South.** City: Hanoi + Ha Giang + Hoi An. **Month: March** (dry, mild nationwide). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* the Ha Giang Loop by motorbike, Halong Bay, street food — active and immersive.

### Age 42 · 2033
- **Tanzania/Rwanda — Gorilla Trekking.** City: Kigali → Volcanoes NP. **Month: June–September** (dry, easier forest trails). **Duration: 8 days.** *Experience:* trekking to mountain gorillas — steep, muddy, altitude; a body-dependent privilege.
- **Morocco — Atlas & Sahara.** City: Marrakech + Toubkal + Merzouga. **Month: April** (wildflowers, climbable High Atlas, bearable desert). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* summit Mt. Toubkal, camel into the Sahara dunes, sleep under the stars.

### Age 43 · 2034
- **Canadian Rockies + Alaska.** City: Banff + Jasper + Denali. **Month: late June–July** (trails clear, wildlife active, long days). **Duration: 16 days.** *Experience:* Lake Louise, the Icefields Parkway, grizzlies, glaciers — big hikes while hiking is easy.
- **Greece — Cyclades island-hopping & hiking.** City: Naxos + Santorini + Folegandros. **Month: late May** (warm, swimmable, pre-crowd). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* coastal trails, ancient ruins, swimming, the Aegean light.

### Age 44 · 2035
- **Mongolia — Gobi & Naadam.** City: Ulaanbaatar + Gobi. **Month: July** (Naadam festival; accessible steppe). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* horse culture, ger camps, the Gobi, the wrestling/archery festival — rugged overlanding.
- **Sri Lanka — Hill Country & Coast.** City: Kandy + Ella + Mirissa. **Month: February** (dry on the south/west). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* tea-country train, Adam's Peak climb, leopards in Yala, whales off Mirissa.

### Age 45 · 2036 — *closing the adventure decade*
- **Tibet + Nepal — Lhasa & Annapurna foothills.** City: Lhasa + Pokhara. **Month: October** (clearest Himalayan season). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* the Potala Palace at altitude, the Tibetan plateau, a shorter Annapurna trek — high-altitude culture while acclimatization is still easy.
- **Tahiti / French Polynesia — diving & lagoons.** City: Bora Bora + Fakarava. **Month: August** (dry, calm, whale season). **Duration: 11 days.** *Experience:* shark-wall dives in Fakarava, overwater lagoons, humpback whales — a reward to end Phase I.

# PHASE II — DEEP IMMERSION (Ages 45–55 · 2036–2046)

> *Theme: trade vertical metres for cultural depth. Slower, longer, richer. You still have the energy for full days — now spend it on history, language, and people instead of altitude.*

### Age 46 · 2037
- **India — Golden Triangle + Rajasthan + Varanasi.** City: Delhi + Jaipur + Udaipur + Varanasi. **Month: November** (post-monsoon, cool, festival season). **Duration: 18 days.** *Experience:* the Taj at dawn, Rajasthan's forts and palaces, the ghats of Varanasi at sunrise — civilizational density.

### Age 47 · 2038
- **Italy — Slow, region by region.** City: Rome + Florence + Naples/Amalfi. **Month: May** (warm, pre-summer crush). **Duration: 21 days.** *Experience:* the Forum, the Uffizi, Pompeii, the Amalfi coast — a long, unhurried immersion in Western art and food.
- **Turkey — Istanbul & Cappadocia.** City: Istanbul + Göreme. **Month: October** (mild, balloon-perfect). **Duration: 11 days.** *Experience:* Hagia Sophia, the Grand Bazaar, the balloon dawn over Cappadocia.

### Age 48 · 2039
- **China — Silk Road & the classics.** City: Beijing + Xi'an + Dunhuang + Guilin. **Month: September–October** (mild, clear). **Duration: 18 days.** *Experience:* the Great Wall, the Terracotta Army, the Mogao Caves, the karst rivers of Guilin.

### Age 49 · 2040
- **Egypt — In depth + Nile cruise.** City: Cairo + Aswan + Abu Simbel. **Month: November.** **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* the Grand Egyptian Museum, a slow Nile cruise, Abu Simbel at dawn — the version of Egypt that rewards patience.
- **Jordan/Israel — the Holy Land & history.** City: Jerusalem + Dead Sea. **Month: March.** **Duration: 10 days.** *Experience:* the layered old city, float in the Dead Sea, Masada at sunrise.

### Age 50 · 2041 — *half-century, a signature long journey*
- **Japan — the full country, slowly.** City: Tokyo + Kyoto + Kanazawa + Naoshima + Hokkaido. **Month: late October–November** (autumn momiji). **Duration: 24 days.** *Experience:* a deep return — art islands, ryokan and onsen, alpine autumn colour, the food at its seasonal best. A landmark trip done at a master's pace.

### Age 51 · 2042
- **Spain & Portugal — the Iberian arc.** City: Madrid + Granada + Lisbon + Porto. **Month: May.** **Duration: 18 days.** *Experience:* the Alhambra, Andalusian flamenco, the Douro valley, Lisbon's light — and optionally a week of the Camino de Santiago while walking is still easy.

### Age 52 · 2043
- **Central Asia — the real Silk Road.** City: Samarkand + Bukhara + Khiva (Uzbekistan). **Month: April or September** (mild shoulder seasons). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* the Registan, turquoise madrasas, caravanserai history — a still-uncrowded marvel best done before infrastructure or knees change.

### Age 53 · 2044
- **Mexico — Maya world & Oaxaca.** City: Oaxaca + Mérida + Yucatán. **Month: late October–early November** (Día de los Muertos). **Duration: 14 days.** *Experience:* Día de los Muertos in Oaxaca, Chichén Itzá and lesser ruins, the world's deepest food culture.
- **Cuba — before it changes.** City: Havana + Trinidad. **Month: February.** **Duration: 8 days.** *Experience:* Havana's music and architecture, a vanishing moment in time.

### Age 54 · 2045
- **Ethiopia — historic north.** City: Lalibela + Gondar + Simien foothills. **Month: October** (post-rains, green, clear). **Duration: 12 days.** *Experience:* the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, Orthodox Timkat-adjacent culture, gelada baboons — demanding logistics, best with mid-life stamina.

### Age 55 · 2046 — *bridge into connoisseurship*
- **Cambodia, Laos & Myanmar (region permitting) — temple circuit.** City: Siem Reap + Luang Prabang. **Month: December–January** (dry, mild). **Duration: 16 days.** *Experience:* sunrise at Angkor Wat, the slow Mekong, Luang Prabang's alms ceremony — spiritual, gentle, profound.

- **A warm-water, single-resort restorative.** City: Maldives, Mauritius, or a comfortable Caribbean base. **Month: dry season for the chosen spot.** **Duration: 10 days.** *Experience:* sun, gentle water, rest, family — restorative simplicity.

## Financial Plan & Per-Trip Budgets (SGD)

> *The headline: you can afford every trip in this plan at a premium tier without ever touching your principal. At ~S$2M growing ~S$500k/year, your wealth is compounding far faster than even a generous travel budget draws down. Travel here is paid for by a slice of your **growth**, not your **capital**.*

### Can you afford it? (yes — comfortably)

- **Your net-worth growth is ~S$500,000/year and rising.** This plan's premium-tier travel averages **~S$55,000/year** over the full 50 years (~S$2.7M lifetime). That's roughly **10–11% of your *annual growth*** in the early years — and a *shrinking* share every year as your wealth compounds.
- **By your 50s–60s**, investment returns alone (on a portfolio likely in the S$10–25M+ range) will exceed your entire annual travel spend several times over. The luxury phases (private rail, expedition suites, river cruises) land exactly when they're most trivially affordable.
- **Rule of thumb to stay guilt-free:** cap travel at **≤15% of each year's net-worth growth**. At today's S$500k growth that's a S$75k/year ceiling — above the ~S$45–55k the early years actually need, leaving headroom for upgrades.

### Recommended tier: "Comfortable Premium" (with Ultra splurges on milestones)

### The funding logic, plainly

1. **Don't budget travel from income — budget it from a dedicated "experiences" sleeve.** Carve out ~S$75k/year (rising with inflation) into a separate liquid bucket. Top it up from annual growth; spend it down without guilt.
2. **Front-load the spend that's tied to your body, not your wallet.** The Phase I adventure trips are the cheapest *and* the most age-sensitive — do them now regardless of where markets are.
3. **Let the luxury phases ride the compounding.** Private rail, suites, and multi-week journeys are deliberately placed in your 50s–70s, when your portfolio makes them rounding errors.
4. **Inflation-index everything.** The SGD figures above are in today's money. At ~3% travel inflation, the same trips cost ~80% more by your 60s — but your wealth will have far outpaced that.
5. **Milestone splurges are pre-approved.** The 40th, 50th, 60th, and 70th flagship trips are where to spend without flinching; they're the memories the peak-end rule says you'll keep.

## A few rules that make this plan actually work

1. **Book the hard trips first and protect those dates ruthlessly.** The Phase I trips are the ones a sprained life schedule will erode. Treat 2026–2036 as a closing window.
2. **Match the month, every time.** The single biggest quality lever after sequencing. The months above are chosen for weather + wildlife + festivals + crowd/cost. Don't drift them for convenience.
3. **One peak per trip.** Design each journey around a single unmistakable high (the summit, the dawn, the dive, the meal) and let the rest support it. You'll remember the peak and the end.
4. **Space them for anticipation.** 2–3 trips/year while young, fewer later. The waiting is part of the happiness — don't compress it away.
5. **Spend asymmetrically.** Splurge on the irreplaceable and one-time (Antarctica, gorillas, a 50th-birthday journey); economize on the repeatable (city breaks, beach resets).
6. **Re-plan every ~5 years.** Health, money, relationships, and the world will change. This is a living document — revise it at 40, 45, 50, and so on.
7. **Travel insurance & health scale with age.** From ~60, factor medical coverage and pace into every booking; from ~75, prioritize direct flights, single bases, and ground-floor access.
