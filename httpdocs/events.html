Aadi Thiruvizha

The Mother's principal festival — a month of intensified worship, abishekams and processions honouring Kali in the sacred month of Aadi.

Navaratri Kolu

Nine nights and nine forms of the Goddess, with the traditional tiered Kolu display, music, and nightly worship culminating in Vijayadashami.
