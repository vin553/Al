Presiding Deity

Sri Veeramakaliamman

வீரமாகாளியம்மன்

"Kali the Courageous" — the temple's presiding Goddess and a fierce form of Shakti. With garland and blade she destroys ego and evil, yet turns boundless maternal love toward her devotees. Prayers here seek courage, protection, and the removal of fear.

Guardian Deity

Sri Periachi

The fierce protector of expectant mothers and newborns. Devotees pray to Periachi Amman for safe childbirth, the wellbeing of children, and protection of the family.

Remover of Obstacles

Lord Vinayagar

The elephant-faced Ganesha, first to be worshipped at any undertaking. Devotees seek his blessing to clear obstacles and bring success to new beginnings.

The Warrior God

Lord Subramaniyar

Murugan, son of Shiva and Parvati, bearer of the divine spear (vel). Lord of courage, wisdom and victory — especially honoured during Thaipusam.

Thaipusam at the temple

The Wide-Eyed Mother

Sri Visalatchi

A gracious form of the Goddess Parvati, consort of Kasi Viswanathar. Worshipped for prosperity, harmony, and family wellbeing.

Protective Devi

Sri Lakshmi Durgai

Durga in her benevolent, prosperity-bestowing aspect — uniting the protective power of Durga with the abundance of Lakshmi. Prayers seek strength, security, and good fortune.

The Supreme Guru

Sri Dhakshinamoorthy

Shiva as the silent teacher, facing south, imparting knowledge through stillness. Students and seekers pray here for wisdom, learning, and clarity of mind.

Lord of Kasi

Sri Kasi Viswanathar

Shiva as worshipped in the holy city of Kasi (Varanasi). Devotees seek liberation, inner peace, and the dissolving of life's burdens.

The Ideal King

Sri Ramar

Lord Rama, avatar of Vishnu, embodiment of righteousness (dharma). Worshipped with Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman for virtue, devotion, and steadfastness.

Devotee-Guardian

Sri Idumbar

The faithful guardian associated with Lord Murugan, honoured for unwavering devotion and service. A reminder that sincere devotion is itself divine.

The Serpent Deity

Sri Nagar

The sacred serpent, associated with fertility, protection, and the removal of naga dosha. Devotees offer prayers for progeny, healing, and prosperity.

Fierce Guardian

Sri Bhairavar

A fierce form of Shiva, guardian of the temple and lord of time. Often worshipped at dusk, he is sought for protection from harm and for justice.

Keeper of Offerings

Sri Chandikeshwarar

The steward of the temple and its offerings, a foremost devotee of Shiva. Worshippers seek his blessing as they conclude their prayers.

The Navagraham

Sri Sani Bagawan

Lord Saturn, among the nine celestial influences (Navagraham). Devotees pray to Sani for relief from hardship, patience, and the lightening of karmic burdens.
